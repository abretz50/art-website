Poetry statement

A poetry statement and featured excerpts are coming soon. Art's creative life has always moved between music and language — growing up playing in band and orchestra, then finding, almost by accident, that he had just as much to say in words. What began as journal entries carried in a notebook during the summer of 2022 gradually became his first poetry manuscript, and that same instinct still shapes his writing today: stillness, spirit, and human feeling, held in the same quiet register as the rest of his creative work.
