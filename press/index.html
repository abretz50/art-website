A tenor, composer, and poet available for your program

Arturo "Art" Tapia-Minchez blends sacred text, personal narrative, and multicultural identity into original choral and vocal work, and performs as a tenor across concert, choral, and operatic settings. Originally from Southern California and now based in Glassboro, New Jersey, he studies Voice Performance and Composition at Rowan University and sings as a rostered vocalist with the Philadelphia Symphonic Chorus. His work is shaped by his own poetry and by his reconnection with Judaism, and he's available for commissions, guest performances, interviews, and feature profiles.

"Just trying to be some light in a whole lotta darkness."
